StarStream maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • Accounts found violating these standards may be suspended or permanently removed.
  • We cooperate with applicable law enforcement authorities where legally required.
